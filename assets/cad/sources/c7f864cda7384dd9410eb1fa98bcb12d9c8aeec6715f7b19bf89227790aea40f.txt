# Genmitsu PROVer V2 Enclosure D

![Concept D CAD assembly](output/EnclosureD.png)

An initial CAD mockup of the selected **D / Fitted Covers** design for an Ursa science workcell based on a stock Genmitsu 3018-PROVer V2. A stationary modular cabinet surrounds independent hollow covers for the gantry, moving head, and moving deck.

**Design and packaging mockup only. Do not print this as a finished kit.** The shell sections are real CAD solids, but joints, retaining strips, mounting holes, fasteners, and load support have not been engineered. The inner machine is schematic reference geometry, not vendor CAD. No protective, containment, clean-environment, or chemical-resistance rating is implied.

## How the covers work

- **Stationary cabinet:** four base tiles, four roof tiles, corner segments, front/side rims and rear panels. It is independent of the moving deck. The rear opening is provisional cable/service access.
- **Fixed gantry:** two open-back pillar covers and two bridge-cover halves conceal brackets and the X structure. The bridge's front slot leaves space for the translating carriage interface.
- **Moving head:** one hollow cover follows the carriage, with rear/bottom access. Its mass, attachment and motor/cable ventilation need design work.
- **Moving deck:** two hollow skirt halves cover the deck perimeter. An illustrative adapter plate covers the work surface; the actual labware layout remains open.
- **Windows:** one clear front sheet and two clear side sheets. Their STL files are shape references for sheet cutting, not instructions to FDM-print transparent panels. The mockup does not yet include retaining strips or latches.

The seams show a possible H2S segmentation, not finished connectors. The roof and front pieces can be hidden individually in CAD to inspect the interior.

## Offline checks and what remains

The exported components are valid single solids with closed STL meshes. The assembly STEP was reloaded and checked for validity and solid count. All 47 printed component bounds fit an H2S orientation; this does not validate supports, warping, tolerances, stiffness, or a sliced print job. The schematic deck and head envelopes do not intersect the stationary cosmetic parts across their modeled travel.

Before making a fit prototype, measure:

1. Deck dimensions and its front/rear extremes, including adapter, clamps, and attachments.
2. Upright profiles, bridge depth/height, motor protrusions, and suitable attachment locations.
3. Installed head/tool at all travel limits and the complete cable/tubing loops.
4. Electronics and emergency-stop access, connector clearances, and ventilation openings.

Then revise parameters and cover geometry, design mounting/retention and structural support, and print small fit coupons. Physical clearance and operation remain operator checks; no hardware was moved for this mockup.

## Rebuild

From the Cubware directory, using the installed FreeCAD runtime:

```sh
/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/python tools/build_genmitsu_enclosure.py --parameters gantry/genmitsu_prover_v2_enclosure_d/parameters.json
/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/python tools/render_genmitsu_enclosure.py --part-previews
/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/python tools/style_genmitsu_enclosure.py
```

The renderer requires a macOS graphics session. Per-part previews use the repository's `scripts/render_preview.py`. Every STL uses assembly coordinates in mm; local print placement/orientation is not supplied.
