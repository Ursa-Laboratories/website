Cub Vernier Go Direct Mount

Install the Vernier Go Direct Force and Acceleration Sensor in the Cub mount, then attach the mount to the Cub gantry’s 3018 holder and Z-axis clamp.

Print notes

The reference print used a Bambu Labs X1-Carbon 3D Printer with Bambu Lab PLA Basic filament. The source instructs the operator to turn support generation on for overhangs. See the illustrated manual below for the slicer settings and assembly orientation.

Mount the sensor

  1. Place the Vernier Go Direct Force and Acceleration Sensor into the rectangular opening of the back half of the mount.
  2. Place the cover onto the assembled sensor and mount.
  3. Place the two M5 × 15mm button head screws through the corresponding holes above the sensor. Place two M5 hex nuts into the hexagonal cavities on the back. Using a 3mm Allen wrench, tighten until snug and clamping the mount around the sensor.
  4. Align the dovetail on the back of the mount base with the dovetail slot on the Cub - 3018 Mount.
  5. Slide the assembly into the circular clamp on the gantry Z-axis. Insert it as far as it will go.
  6. Using the 3mm Allen wrench, tighten the circular-clamp screw until the assembly is snug. Plug the provided cable into the side of the Vernier sensor.
